Dimension 1: Financial Performance & Growth (PRD 10.1)
This dimension tests whether the company's historical growth is real and if their future forecasting is trustworthy.

Question ID: FIN_PERF_01

Question Text: "What is the variance percentage between the board-approved annual revenue budget and the actual recognized revenue over the trailing 12 months?"

Data Proof / Input Type: PERCENTAGE (Verified via Board Decks & Audited P&L).

Risk Weight: HIGH

Red Flag Threshold: Consistently missing forecasts by more than 15% indicates poor management visibility.

Remediation Action (90 Days): "Implement a rolling 13-week cash flow forecast and transition from annual budgeting to quarterly re-forecasting."

Question ID: FIN_PERF_02

Question Text: "What is the total dollar amount of non-recurring management 'add-backs' to EBITDA (e.g., personal expenses, one-time legal fees, owner salaries)?"

Data Proof / Input Type: CURRENCY (Verified via Quality of Earnings schedule).

Risk Weight: CRITICAL_DEALBREAKER

Red Flag Threshold: Add-backs exceeding 10% of total reported EBITDA often cause investors to walk away or renegotiate valuation.

Remediation Action (60 Days): "Engage a third-party CPA to conduct an independent Quality of Earnings (QoE) audit to normalize EBITDA before opening the data room."

Dimension 2: Profitability & Cash Flow (PRD 10.2)
This dimension ensures the company will not go bankrupt during the diligence process.

Question ID: CASH_FLOW_01

Question Text: "What is the average monthly net cash burn over the last 6 months, and exactly how many months of cash runway remain under current balances?"

Data Proof / Input Type: NUMBER (Verified via Bank Statements & Cash Flow Statement).

Risk Weight: CRITICAL_DEALBREAKER

Red Flag Threshold: Less than 6 to 9 months of runway completely destroys the company's negotiating leverage with investors.

Remediation Action (30 Days): "Execute an immediate hiring freeze and freeze all non-essential CapEx to extend runway past the 9-month threshold."

Question ID: CASH_FLOW_02

Question Text: "What is the company's gross margin percentage, fully burdened by customer success, hosting, and implementation costs?"

Data Proof / Input Type: PERCENTAGE (Verified via trailing 12-month P&L).

Risk Weight: MEDIUM

Remediation Action (180 Days): "Conduct a pricing audit to pass inflationary costs to customers and renegotiate vendor/hosting contracts to expand gross margins."

Dimension 3: Revenue Quality & Concentration (PRD 10.3)
This dimension tests if the company's revenue is highly vulnerable or highly durable.

Question ID: REV_QUAL_01

Question Text: "What percentage of total gross revenue is generated by the Top 1, Top 3, and Top 5 largest customers?"

Data Proof / Input Type: PERCENTAGE (Verified via Accounts Receivable Aging Report).

Risk Weight: CRITICAL_DEALBREAKER

Red Flag Threshold: A single customer accounting for >20% of total revenue is a massive single-point-of-failure risk.

Remediation Action (180 Days): "Restructure sales incentives to prioritize net-new logos in adjacent market segments to dilute Top 5 customer concentration."

Question ID: REV_QUAL_02

Question Text: "What percentage of total revenue is contractually recurring (e.g., multi-year subscriptions) versus one-off, project-based transactional revenue?"

Data Proof / Input Type: PERCENTAGE (Verified via Customer CRM Export).

Risk Weight: HIGH

Remediation Action (90 Days): "Transition existing transactional/per-project clients to annual retainer contracts with automatic renewal clauses."

Question ID: REV_QUAL_03

Question Text: "What is the Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and Gross Dollar Retention (GDR) across historical customer cohorts over the last 24 months?"

Data Proof / Input Type: PERCENTAGE (Verified via Cohort Analysis).

Risk Weight: HIGH

Red Flag Threshold: NRR below 100% means the company's existing customer base is shrinking faster than it is expanding.

Remediation Action (60 Days): "Implement a dedicated Customer Success up-sell matrix and enforce 90-day preemptive check-ins prior to contract renewal dates."
